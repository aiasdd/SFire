## Brand Kit
### Microsoft 365 Copilot Brand Kit에 맞춰서 추출 (in Cowork)
**You will help me build a Microsoft 365 Copilot Brand Kit by producing two deliverables: a brand guidelines document and a presentation template, both ready to upload to a new brand kit. Work through the phases below in order. Show your reasoning briefly at each phase before acting.**

**Phase 1 — Research the target system**

- Research Microsoft 365 Copilot Brand Kits and brand guidelines.
- Use web\_search to find Microsoft’s current official documentation on Copilot Brand Kits (accepted formats, required vs. optional fields, logo constraints, color definitions, fonts, templates).
- Use SearchM365 for internal references to "brand kit", "brand guidelines", or "Copilot brand".
- Summarize: (a) exact fields/assets required (b) recommended structure of brand guidelines document (c) recommended structure of presentation template
- Cite sources. Flag confidence below 75 and note what to verify.

**Phase 2 — Locate and analyze the source presentation**

- Check Glob for source deck. If multiple PPTXs exist, list them; if none, ask for upload.
- Extract from deck:
    - Logo files and placement patterns
    - Full color palette (hex, RGB, usage frequency, primary vs. accent)
    - Typography (heading font, body font, sizes, weights, fallbacks)
    - Imagery style (photography, iconography, illustration treatment)
    - Layout and grid patterns (margins, spacing, composition)
    - Voice and tone signals (taglines, headlines, body language)
    - Explicit brand statements, mission, positioning text
- Produce a structured "brand audit" summary.

**Phase 3 — Map findings to brand guideline requirements**

- Cross-reference Phase 1 (requirements) with Phase 2 (deck contents).
- Build a gap table:
    - Required field → Found in deck? → Source slide/element → Confidence
- For gaps: use placeholders like [Add tagline], [Confirm minimum logo size]. Do not fabricate brand facts.

**Phase 4 — Generate the brand guidelines document**

- Create **brand-guidelines.docx** with sections:
    - Brand overview — mission, positioning, audience (placeholders if missing)

**Brand System Elements**

- **Logo system** — variants, clear space, minimum size, do/don’t usage
- **Color palette** — primary, secondary, accent with hex/RGB and usage rules
- **Typography** — heading and body fonts, scale, weights, fallbacks
- **Voice & tone** — attributes, do/don’t language, sample copy
- **Imagery & iconography** — photo treatment, illustration style, icon principles
- **Layout & grid** — spacing system, composition principles, slide examples
- Use actual brand colors as section accents. Embed extracted logo and palette swatches.

**Phase 5 — Generate the presentation template**

- Create **brand-template.pptx** with:
    - Master slides reflecting extracted color theme and font scheme
    - Title slide, section divider, content (1-col, 2-col), image+text, full-image, closing slide layouts
    - Placeholder text in extracted voice/tone style
    - Logo placement on appropriate masters
    - No source content — empty, reusable shells only
- Verify file presence in output/.

**Phase 6 — Reflect and report**

- Summarize: what was extracted, what was placeholder’d, confidence on alignment to Copilot Brand Kit requirements, and exact upload steps for each deliverable.
- Flag any item below 75 confidence with recommended verification path.
- Prefer retrieval over assumption, cite sources, never fabricate brand facts.
- Use TaskCreate to track each phase for progress visibility.
